@startuml
skinparam monochrome true
skinparam shadowing false
skinparam defaultFontName Arial

title Activity Diagram - Hospital Management System (Patient Flow & Billing)

|Patient|
start
:Fill Registration Details;

|Receptionist|
:Verify Identity & Register;
:Check Doctor Availability;

if (Slot Available?) then (Yes)
  :Issue Appointment Token;
else (No)
  :Propose Alternate Slot;
  if (Patient Accepts?) then (Yes)
    :Issue Appointment Token;
  else (No)
    :Cancel Booking;
    stop
  endif
endif

|Doctor|
:Examine Patient & Vitals;
:Enter Diagnosis in EHR;

fork
  :Order Diagnostic Lab Tests;
  |Diagnostic Lab|
  :Collect Specimen Sample;
  :Analyze & Upload Lab Report;
fork again
  :Write Electronic Prescription;
  |Pharmacy|
  :Verify Medicine Stock;
  :Dispense Prescribed Drugs;
end fork

|Billing System|
:Aggregate Consultation, Lab & Drug Costs;
:Execute calculateBill();

if (Has Insurance OR (Emergency AND Bill > 5000)?) then (Yes)
  :Apply Discount / Co-pay Waiver;
else (No)
  :Retain Standard Gross Amount;
endif

|Patient|
:Choose Payment Mode (Card / UPI / Cash);
:Authorize Payment Transaction;

|Billing System|
if (Payment Successful?) then (Yes)
  :Update Ledger to PAID;
  :Generate Itemized Invoice & Receipt;
  |Patient|
  :Collect Receipt & Discharged;
  stop
else (No)
  :Flag Transaction Failed;
  |Patient|
  :Retry Payment with Alternate Mode;
  detach
endif

@enduml
